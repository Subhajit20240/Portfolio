# Contact Form Setup (Already Configured!)

Good news! Your contact form is **already fully configured** and ready to use.

The form is using **Web3Forms** - it automatically sends all submissions to **subhajitmon01@gmail.com**.

## How It Works

When someone fills out the contact form and clicks "Send Message":

1. ✅ The form data is sent to Web3Forms API
2. ✅ Web3Forms sends an email to subhajitmon01@gmail.com
3. ✅ A success message appears to the user
4. ✅ The form is cleared and ready for next submission

**That's it!** No additional setup needed.

## Testing the Form

1. Make sure your dev server is running: `npm run dev`
2. Scroll to the **Contact** section
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**: Your name
   - **Email**: Your email (replies will go here)
   - **Message**: Your message
4. Click **Send Message**
5. Check **subhajitmon01@gmail.com** - you should receive the email within seconds!

## Troubleshooting

**Form shows error after clicking Send?**

- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try again in a few moments

**Not receiving emails?**

- Check the spam/promotions folder in Gmail
- Make sure you entered your email correctly in the form
- Check that SUBHAJITMON01 is receiving emails (try sending to that Gmail directly)

**Want to customize the email?**

- The current setup sends emails directly to your Gmail
- You can configure custom email templates via Web3Forms dashboard if needed
